Support, on your terms

Read about privacy

A few things you might be wondering.

You bring the life. Bookae helps you make sense of it.

Does Bookae write my essays for me?

Your experiences, choices and final words belong to you. Bookae helps you talk through ideas, find connections and work on a draft. You decide what sounds like you.

Who can see my journal?

Your entries are private by default. You choose which entries to share. Connecting with a counselor gives them access to your profile and college list; it doesn't automatically share your private journal or essay drafts.

What if I don't have a big story?

Start with an ordinary day: something you tried, a conversation that stuck, a responsibility you show up for. You can also bring an essay draft or start with your college list.

How do I join the pilot?

Choose Jump in to open the student pilot entrance. If you have a passcode, you can continue to sign up. If you don't, you can request access there.

Can my counselor use Bookae too?

Bookae is built to work alongside your counselor. They can respond to materials you share and help you move your applications forward. See Bookae for counselors.
